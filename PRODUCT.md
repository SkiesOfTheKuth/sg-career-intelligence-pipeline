# SG Career Intelligence

## Register

product

## Users

Dechsurang uses one private workspace to find credible Singapore vacancies, prepare applications, and manage follow-ups.

## Product Purpose

Support discovery, evidence-based assessment, preparation, and durable application tracking. The implementation plan supplied by the user is the approved product brief.

## Brand Personality

Calm, factual, personal.

## Anti-references

Avoid executive dashboards, metric tiles, nested cards, invented percentages, repeated badges, and generic strategic advice.

## Design Principles

- Show employer evidence before candidate advice; missing facts remain unknown.
- Two destinations: Find roles and Applications.
- Preserve edits and history, including unavailable roles.
- Use specific actions and explicit application submission tracking.
- Keep search and useful content immediately visible on phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Label controls, manage detail-view focus, support keyboard operation and reduced motion, maintain readable contrast and 44px touch targets. Verify 390px, 768px, 1440px, and 200% zoom.

## Existing visual identity

Retain readable sans-serif typography and the existing blue accent (#2563EB), using simple separators and restrained surfaces.
